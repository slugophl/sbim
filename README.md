# Small Business Invoice Management
FinTech application for small business invoice management.

The Small Business Invoice Management application will give small businesses a simple and cost-effective way to manage their invoices. It will help small businesses stay healthy and prosperous by:
- Reducing human error
- Receiving payments in a timely manner
- Eliminating duplicate invoices

Essential features of the application include:
* Create invoices via user data input
* List invoices
* Update/Delete invoices (i.e., mark as paid)
* Email notification for upcoming due dates

Samuel Lugo will be responsible for development, validation and presentation of all essential features.
